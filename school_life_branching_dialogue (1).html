Different routes · about 5–10 minutes
Your situation

Your project was due today, but it is not finished. You have completed the research and still need to write the final section. You have two tests this week. You can finish the project by Friday.

How to play

Choose the response that best fits the situation and shows respectful, helpful communication. Your choices change both the teacher’s next question and the responses available to you.

Some responses contain correct English but are unsuitable for this conversation. Choose one response at each step. Check your answers at the end.

You can go back and change a response. Changing an earlier response restarts the conversation from that point.
